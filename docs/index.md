## Hello! 

<img src="assets/headshot.jpeg" alt="Photo of Hannah" style="width: 30%; float: right; margin: 2%">

I'm a software engineer at Microsoft currently working on services supporting datacenter operations. I'm a recent graduate of the University of Washington, where I received my masters degree in computer science. I also completed my bachelors degree in computer science and applied mathematics with College Honors at UW. 

I'm broadly interested in responsible AI, learning how machine learning systems harm people and what steps we might take to mitigate these harms. Before joining Microsoft, I was a machine learning engineer at [TrueMedia.org](https://www.truemedia.org) where I tackled deepfake detection as a continuation of [research]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link research.md %}) I conducted at UW for my masters degree. Before that, I researched [computer vision techniques for mobile health applications]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link research.md %}), often encountering questions of fairness. 

Other long term interests of mine include [running](https://hannahyklee.github.io/running/2025/03/10/years-in-running.html), hiking, reading, and drawing ([often]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link play.md %}) of places I've run in or hiked around!).

This site (attempts to) organize my notes, updates, and miscellaneous digital artifacts. It is very much a work in progress!
